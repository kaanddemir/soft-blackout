# Changelog

## 2.0.0 — October 2026

Soft Blackout has been rebuilt around three modes you direct yourself.

- **Manual** masks exactly the text you select, after you confirm it. **Reveal** removes part of a mask. **Mask on select** skips the confirmation for mouse selections.
- **Poetry** lets you choose the words to keep in a paragraph and hides the rest. Applied poems can be edited.
- **Pattern Mask** finds email addresses, URLs and, optionally, phone numbers. You review the counts before anything is masked.
- **Solid** and **Marker** styles in five colours. Changing them restyles every mask on the page.
- One Undo and Redo history across all three modes, plus **Reset page**.
- Masks no longer change the page's text or structure. The original text stays in place and is drawn over.
- New popup design with System, Light and Dark themes. The on-page controls follow the same theme.
- Keyboard support for every control, and masks are refused rather than shown incorrectly when Windows High Contrast is on.
- Quick Redact, Blackout All, the Privacy and Random redaction modes, intensity levels and automatic word selection have been removed. Poetry now keeps only the words you choose.
- The popup is English only. Requires Chrome 116 or newer. Permissions are unchanged.
